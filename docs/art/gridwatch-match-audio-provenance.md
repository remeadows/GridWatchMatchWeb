# GridWatch Match Board Audio Provenance

Approved family: Tactical Glass (Candidate A), 2026-07-16.

All source clips are from Kenney's official CC0 1.0 audio packs: Digital Audio,
Sci-Fi Sounds, Impact Sounds, and Interface Sounds. Every installed clip was
trimmed for its board-event role, rendered at 48 kHz stereo MP3 / 160 kbps, high
pass filtered at 55 Hz, low pass filtered at 18 kHz, attenuated to 55%, and
limited to -1.1 dBFS. No Royal Match audio or other copyrighted game audio was
used.
